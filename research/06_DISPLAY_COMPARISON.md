# 06 — HoloDisplay: a contrast case in evidence

The CES preparation slides and presentation script (D02, D03) contain a second track: quantum-computing fundamentals and Samsung's floating-display concept. This case tests whether the same evidence discipline transfers to a different technology.

## What can be established

The CES organizer lists HoloDisplay as 2025 Best of Innovation in Smart Home and an Honoree in Home Appliances (S18). Its description identifies aerial-display technology and advanced sensors. Samsung's own CES report also describes a floating-display showcase (S19).

These sources establish an award record and a presented concept. The organizer's description includes submitted promotional claims; it is not an independent laboratory result.

## Claims that need narrowing

| Script claim | Evidence treatment | Better research question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “World first” | Attribute to product description rather than establish priority | What prior-art search and category definition support the claim? |
| Micro LED creates the aerial image | Not established by the retrieved entry | What optical architecture and emitter are actually used? |
| No hygiene problem | Non-contact interaction does not demonstrate a hygiene outcome | Does it reduce contact or contamination in controlled use? |
| Glasses-free 3D movies | Not established by these sources | What depth cues, viewing zone and content formats are supported? |
| Broad commercialization | Availability and price not established | What shipping product, distribution and support exist? |

## Technical-commercial test

**Project interpretation:** the buyer problem is interaction while hands are occupied or dirty. Compare the concept against voice control, a conventional display, a tablet and physical controls. A striking image is not enough if lighting, steam, viewing position or gesture errors limit use.

A proposed study would measure task completion, mistaken activation, readability across viewing angles, performance under relevant lighting/steam conditions, cleaning requirements and willingness to pay. Define safe-use conditions with the operator; do not assume a floating control is appropriate near every appliance.

No optical bench experiment or user study has been run here. The contribution is a testable evaluation design.

## Repair the quantum introduction

D03's “0 and 1 simultaneously” shorthand needs an explanation of measurement. IBM's fundamentals material (S20) explains that measurement does not reveal every possible answer. **Interpretation:** quantum advantage must be argued for a specified algorithm and problem, not generalized into a claim that all computing becomes faster.

This clarification matters to the PQC track: a cryptanalytic threat concerns specific mathematical problems under sufficiently capable hardware, not a generic speed multiplier.

## Cross-case conclusion

PQC and floating displays have different customers, engineering constraints and evidence requirements. Their useful connection is methodological: trace the mechanism, separate demonstration from validation, and require evidence for the proposed benefit. No single market-size estimate combines them.

# 02 — Where value may accrue

## Commercial hypothesis

**Project interpretation:** initial customer value is likely to be concentrated where sensitive information lasts a long time, systems are difficult to replace, and buyers can fund integration. This is a demand hypothesis, not a market-size estimate.

## Segment the work before estimating revenue

| Work category | Buyer problem | Observable purchase evidence | Substitution risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory and discovery | Unknown cryptographic dependencies | Coverage by system owner, protocol and key purpose | Existing security tooling may add discovery |
| Migration integration | Incompatible libraries, certificates or appliances | Successful staged deployment with documented rollback | Platform vendors may supply upgrades |
| Orchestration | Policies and algorithms change across many systems | Verified policy changes and exception management | Native platform control planes may suffice |
| Assurance and testing | A “PQC” label gives little operational confidence | Independent scope-specific tests and reproducible artifacts | Buyer may prefer internal validation |
| Managed operations | Limited specialist capacity | Service responsibilities, key custody and recovery terms | Outsourcing creates new trust dependencies |

## A bottom-up model without invented inputs

A future estimate can use:

`annual opportunity = eligible organizations × reachable share × paid conversion × annual contract value`

Separate recurring software from one-time integration fees. Each factor needs an independent evidence source and a defined geography, buyer segment and year. Avoid multiplying a broad cybersecurity market by an arbitrary PQC percentage.

| Input | Present evidence | Current treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible organizations | No bounded customer census | Unestimated |
| Reachable share | No verified sales channel coverage | Unestimated |
| Paid conversion | No qualified pipeline or contract data | Unestimated |
| Contract value | No representative pricing sample | Unestimated |
| Retention and expansion | No customer cohort data | Unestimated |

No revenue forecast or CAGR is reported. “Unestimated” is more informative than a precise number without a defensible denominator.

## Scenario logic

**Platform absorption:** standardized capabilities arrive through existing infrastructure. Independent vendors must prove benefits beyond the upgrade itself.

**Integration bottleneck:** heterogeneous estates require discovery, coordination and testing. Specialist providers compete on execution and assurance.

**Delayed procurement:** buyers acknowledge risk but postpone spending. Demonstrations and awards fail to convert into recurring contracts.

Track evidence that distinguishes these scenarios: budget ownership, paid production scope, renewal behavior and deployment completion. Announcements alone cannot separate them.

## Commercial decision gate

A credible opportunity memo needs a named buyer problem, alternatives, budget evidence, implementation cost and a measurable reason to choose the product. Technical urgency and commercial traction are separate propositions.

# Research protocol

Review date: 2026-10-29. Scope: post-quantum cryptography (PQC), using a CES 2025 presentation as the starting question rather than as proof of product maturity.

## Research question

When does a quantum-security claim become a credible migration proposition for a buyer with existing infrastructure?

The working thesis is that the strongest near-term proposition is measurable migration capability: discovering cryptographic dependencies, replacing vulnerable primitives, and sustaining interoperability. A new algorithm or a quantum-themed brand alone does not establish customer value.

## Evidence contract

| Label | Meaning | Permitted conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Normative publication from its issuing body | What the specified primitive does, within its stated assumptions |
| Research | Original paper or technical experiment | What was established under the reported model or test setup |
| Institutional analysis | Public-sector or central-bank research | A framework or observed implementation constraint |
| Analyst assessment | Public analyst publication | That analyst's assessment, with scope and commercial context retained |
| Vendor claim | Company-controlled communication | What the vendor asserts; not independent validation |
| Project interpretation | Reasoning developed in this repository | A conditional conclusion that may be challenged |
| Unresolved | Missing or insufficient evidence | No affirmative capability claim |

## Time discipline

The event label is CES 2025. It is not the date of this repository's analysis. The supplied deck was read on 2026-10-29; provider metadata reported creation on 2026-10-21 and modification on 2026-10-28. Its presentation date, earlier versions, authorship contributions and event attendance are not established by that metadata.

Sources published before January 2025 form background available before the event. Later sources are explicitly retrospective follow-up. Live vendor pages are current observations, never reconstructed 2025 product specifications. A source publication date and its retrieval date must be recorded separately.

## Selection and exclusion

Search follows the deck's actual topic: standards, algorithm design, system integration, QuSecure and Quranium, then commercial demand. Prefer original issuers, original researchers and identifiable analyst documents. Retain counterevidence and gaps. Exclude unattributed market-size numbers, undated claims presented as historical facts, unverifiable deployments, and security absolutes.

The corpus is purposive, not a systematic review or exhaustive market census. It can support a bounded thesis, not a measured global market share.

## Update rule

Every material conclusion needs a source ID from [the source register](../sources/REGISTER.md), an explicit interpretation label, or an unresolved status. Each update must review the thesis, contrary evidence, company notes and README together. New evidence may narrow or reverse a conclusion. No benchmark, customer interview, code execution against a vendor product, or original cryptographic implementation is claimed here.
